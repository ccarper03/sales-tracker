# Sales Tracker

A phone tracker for new home sales reps. Each rep's numbers stay on their own phone.
Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody else can see them.

Open `charlescarper.com/sales-tracker/` on your phone, then Share, Add to Home Screen.

## What you log

- **Log tab.** Activities, appointments set, no-shows, reschedules, cancels, appointments held,
  walk-ins, and sales (first visit or be-back, with price and presale or spec).
  Objections: tap Faced when one comes up, Beat when you work through it. Every tap can be undone.
  Use the arrows to fix an earlier day.
- **Month tab.** Show-up rate, close rate, sales, estimated commission, objections beaten by type,
  and goal math: change your sales goal and see the visits and appointments it takes.
- **Settings.** Your name, commission rates, weekly activity floor, default goal.
  Export or import a CSV. Erase everything.

## How the numbers work

| Number | Math |
|---|---|
| Show-up rate | Appointments held ÷ appointments set |
| Close rate | Sales ÷ (appointments held + walk-ins) |
| Est. commission | Each sale's price × presale or spec rate (defaults 2% and 1.75%) |
| Visits needed | Sales still needed ÷ close rate |
| Appointments to set | (Visits needed − walk-ins expected for the rest of the month at your pace) ÷ show-up rate |
| Activities this week | Monday through Sunday, against your weekly floor (default 50) |

## Keep a copy

Data lives in the phone's browser. Clearing Safari's website data, or switching phones, erases it.
Export a CSV now and then. Import it on the new phone to pick up where you left off.

Adapted from a "Main Data Tracker" sales sheet, reworked for in-person new home sales.
